## Systematic issues

1. **Particle restrictions stated too absolutely**: 이 아니다 ("never 은/는" — contrastive “학생은 아니에요” is normal),
   만 vs 밖에 (usage contradicted its own compare), 부터 never with places (“여기부터”), 에 never after “그때”.
2. **Topic/subject**: the 은/는 lesson did not warn about the commonest real error, a topic inside a modifier clause
   (“이것은 저는 어제 산 책이에요”); added. The contrastive “지수는 왔어요” was translated as a plain topic in two lessons.
3. **Form slips**: “알겠어요” listed as a past form (-었-), the ungrammatical pair “내일 갔어요” used as a compare example,
   “감사해서” called a fixed phrase.
4. **Written vs spoken overclaims**: -어야 되다 = "spoken", -어야 하다 = "written"; 이다 dropping its 이 "only in speech and
   informal writing" (standard in all writing: “의사다”).
5. **English**: “sir” invented a gender (-습니까); scattered tense and logic slips in translations.

The 이다 lesson (written last) was checked line by line: 이에요/예요, 입니다, 이었다/였다, 아니다 with 이/가, spacing, and the
three pitfalls are correct; the only fixes were the vowel-drop register claim, the added “아니에요” spelling note and the TIP.
