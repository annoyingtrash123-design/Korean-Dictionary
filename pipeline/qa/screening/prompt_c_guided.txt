CHECKLIST (from past screens: the errors most often missed were wrong word sense, idioms read literally, who-did-what, quantities and verb endings; fluent English is NOT evidence that it is right):
A. Read the Korean BEFORE the English. Work out each clause: who (이/가/은/는), does what, to whom (을/를), with whom (와/과: 나와 = "with me"), where/when (에/에서/으로), and what each modifier attaches to (모두/다/만, ~의, -ㄴ/-는 clauses). Only then compare the English clause by clause.

H. People (MINOR): surname + ssi = "Kim" (not "Mr. Kim"); surname + 모 + ssi = "a certain Kim"; 노인/젊은이/아이/친구/선생님/주인공 have no sex; no invented his/her. 그 (pronoun) = he and 그녀 = she (MAJOR if rendered "them" or "a man"); 그 + noun = "that …".
