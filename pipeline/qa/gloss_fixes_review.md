## 단연 (42364/0) — 2026-11-19 — independent review

**Decision: approve as is** — new_gloss `by far; decidedly; clearly` (old: `obviously`).

- krdict ko_def (순위나 수준, 정도 등이 확실히 판단이 될 만큼 뚜렷하게) and every krdict example (단연 돋보이다/뛰어나다/앞서다/우세하다/으뜸이다/제일이다/최고이다) are rank/comparison contexts; 표준 斷然 (확실히 단정할 만하게) has the same examples (단연 앞선다, 단연 으뜸이었다). "by far" is the natural English for these ("단연 최고" = by far the best) and matches the existing example translation.
- "obviously" is misleading rather than outright false: in English it is mainly a sentence adverb of self-evidence ("Obviously, he'll come" = 당연히/분명히), which 단연 never expresses; it also misses the margin-of-superiority nuance.
- "decidedly" (decidedly superior) and "clearly" (clearly the best / stands out clearly) both fit as degree adverbs modifying comparatives/superlatives; "clearly" adds no new sense here, since the ko_def itself says 뚜렷하게. Ordering with "by far" first steers learners to the comparative use.
- Format matches krdict style (short items, "; " separators, no trailing punctuation).
