## Systematic issues

1. **한테/에게 with 오다 taught as a strict direction rule.** 에게서 and 한테서 both claimed “친구한테 전화가 왔다” means a call
   came *to* the friend; in speech it normally means *from* the friend (the 한테서 lesson's own TIP said so). Now presented as
   a written-language distinction that speech blurs.
2. **Absolute restrictions with common counter-examples**: “보내다” never with 에게로 (krdict example has it), adjectives with
   “-ㄴ/은 동안”, -으면서 never with a tense ending (“알았으면서도”), -었다가 only for reverse actions.
3. **Compare pairs that do not isolate the contrast**: -은3's adjective-vs-verb pair used two verbs; -거나/-든지 compared a
   statement with a doubled command; 부터/에서부터 used an odd sentence.
4. **Curriculum sense coverage**: -지 (서술, 물음, 명령, 요청) lacked the command and request uses although its TIP claimed
   all four; -는데2 (감탄) had a non-exclamative warning example; -음 lacked the written clause-nominaliser use.
5. **Spacing of dictionary compounds**: 올려놓다, 물어보다, 찾아보다, 도와주다 were written as auxiliary constructions.
6. Pitfalls that are not real learner errors (“내일 가는 기로 했다”) replaced with real ones (“-기로 해요” for a decision made).
